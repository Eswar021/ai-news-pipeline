# Editorial Signal

One episode must feel as if it was cut by one editor. Keep the warm paper
canvas, near-black ink, burnt-orange accent, serif display type, and condensed
sans labels across every recipe.

## Frame Rules

- Build a visual moment, not a web page or dashboard.
- Use one dominant focal point and one supporting focal point.
- Keep headlines large and complete. Never display arbitrary narration slices.
- Treat real source pages, images, video, and identity marks as the visual
  subject; annotations explain them without covering them.
- Code-generated explainers use two to four large elements and one clear motion
  path. They do not use card grids, tiny labels, or decorative metrics.
- Use restrained paper texture, rules, and registration marks for depth. Do not
  switch to a dark tech theme inside an episode.

## Motion Rules

- Choreograph every recipe with a short establish, a focused reveal, and a
  settled hold that remains readable under narration.
- Prefer wipes, draws, focus pushes, countups, and source scans over generic
  fade-up animation.
- Keep all motion deterministic and seek-safe on one paused GSAP timeline.
